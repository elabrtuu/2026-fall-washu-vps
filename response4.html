Media Response

"Eno"

I thought this documentary was thoughtfully curated, and I really liked the idea that it regenerates its own structure and order of clips at every screening. I thought that that was a concept that really works with these clips, since they are all pretty vague and abstract, and the order in which they are presented can really change the way that they are interpreted. I also liked how the documentary was able to show the different ways that Eno has been able to create music, and how he has been able to use different techniques to create different sounds. I thought that the documentary was able to provide a true insight into Eno's ways and his creative process, and it was a really interesting way to learn about his work.
