Reading Response 5

"The Forbidden Zone"

The thing that stood out to me is Damon Zucconi's idea that if you build a container, you'll make things to fill it, which reframes a website as a starting point instead of a final display. I also liked his point that a container only works if it shows up in your daily life, like his random morning text or his new-tab page of image pairs. I thought the “forbidden zone” concept ties it together: there will always be far more out there than we can ever encounter, so the containers we build decide what we notice. It makes collecting feel less like hoarding and more like setting up your own luck.
