Media Response

"There are No Gardens on the Internet"

This website made me rethink the cozy “digital garden” idea. I like the idea that a garden only feels peaceful because of its fence, and the metaphor hides the labor and materials that actually make the internet run. I also liked that the piece uses clickable hypertext and a collected reading list to pull you out past the garden walls instead of keeping you comfortable inside them. Overall, I thought it was a thoughtful and brilliant way to go beyond the boundaries of what a website is, and explored the idea of what a website can be.
