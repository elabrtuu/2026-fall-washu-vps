Reading Response 2

"The New Turing Test"

This commentary on AI was thoughtful and very relevant to the role of AI in today's world. I appreciated how she reframed the conversation from trying to find a presence of AI in her students' work to instead evaluating the expressiveness and human capabilities showcase through their work. She emphasized this by stating that "art is meant to form connections", and followed up by saying that "something created without AI can still be inexpressive" just as well as "something created with AI can be expressive". Overall, the article reframes the priority in which art and creative work should be evaluated, which I think is a very important distinction to make in today's world where AI is taking up more and more space.
